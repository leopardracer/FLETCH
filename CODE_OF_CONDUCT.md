# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
FLETCH project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users, not fabricated
  claims about what the software does (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s one hard
  rule — the same honesty standard applies to how we talk to each other)

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal/political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainer by opening a
[GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/leopardracer/FLETCH/security/advisories/new)
(private) or, for non-security conduct reports, by contacting the
maintainer directly via their GitHub profile. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
