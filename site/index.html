A price is the result. FLETCH reads what caused it, straight from Robinhood Chain and the Pons V2 launchpad — no scraped lists, no seed data, nothing invented.

Trades

Holders

FAQ

What is FLETCH?

A discovery feed, a risk engine and an AI explanation layer for meme tokens on Robinhood Chain. It reads launches, trades, holders, liquidity and wallet behavior directly off the chain.

Where can I use it?

At app.getfletch.xyz — free, no sign-up, no wallet connection. Ask FLETCH AI works with your own Anthropic key, which stays in your browser tab.

Where does the data come from?

Direct reads against Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663) and the Pons V2 launchpad contracts. Every launch FLETCH sees is remembered, and holders and trades are tracked block by block from launch.

Does FLETCH predict prices?

No. FLETCH AI explains and answers; it never predicts. It only sees facts FLETCH's engine already computed from the chain, and it says plainly when data isn't available.

What does the FLETCH Score measure?

An explainable blend of momentum, liquidity, holder growth, whale activity and safety — weighted across only the parts that are actually available for that token.

How is Radar different from the score?

Radar ranks by how much is changing right now — recent signals converging on one token — not by the score or the token's size.

Can FLETCH trade for me?

No. FLETCH is read-only. There is no way in the code to sign or send a transaction.

Is it open source?

Yes — MIT licensed, on GitHub, with its full test suite.
